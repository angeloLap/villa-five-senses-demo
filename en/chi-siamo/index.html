Our story

Villa Five Senses is a home restaurant set in the beautiful countryside of the province of Taranto, where a love of traditional Puglian, Lucanian and southern Italian cooking meets the warm hospitality of the South.

Our kitchen

Every week we offer a menu that celebrates local produce, respecting the seasons and highlighting Puglian, Lucanian and Russian culinary traditions.

Here you will find the authentic flavours of the past, prepared with care and dedication.
